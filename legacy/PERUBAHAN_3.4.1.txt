Dashboard Cuaca Aziz 3.4.1 - Senarai perubahan / Changes
=========================================================
Asas: 3.4.0 + laporan 19 Nov 2026: "Uji relay" di PC -> "✗ Relay failed: cannot be reached".

PUNCA (pepijat app, bukan persediaan Cloudflare)
   Atas sebab keselamatan, halaman app desktop hanya dibenarkan bercakap dengan PC sendiri. Butang "Uji relay" di PC cuba
   menghubungi workers.dev terus dari halaman itu, jadi permintaan disekat sebelum keluar. (Di iPad / telefon tiada sekatan ini.)
   Ujian 3.4.0 terlepas kerana ia dijalankan dalam pelayar biasa, bukan dalam app desktop.

PEMBETULAN
   Di PC, "Uji relay" kini dijalankan oleh bahagian utama app (seperti pengumpul data). Sekatan keselamatan halaman kekal.

DIUJI
   - App Electron sebenar: 46/46 (baru: Uji relay dari app desktop - alamat salah = "cannot be reached", kunci salah dikesan,
     relay dengan kunci betul dapat dicapai).
   - tests/unit.js 33/33, Playwright 19/19.

EN: "Test relay" on the PC always said "cannot be reached": the app page may only talk to the PC itself, so the request to
workers.dev was blocked. The desktop app now runs the test in its main process. Electron 46/46, unit 33/33, browser 19/19.
